# Composition (approved)

## The fixed frame

The page is built around one sticky element, **the band**. The band holds the gauntlet and travels with the visitor through every beat except the colophon.

**Band geometry at 1440 px:**

- **Language zone.** Columns 1–3 of the 12-column grid. The gutter is 24 px and the outer margin is 48 px.
- **Gauntlet.** Columns 4–9.
  - The 11 gates are evenly spaced, with the LLM gate 32 px before G1.
  - The 4 depth gates come after G11 at 50% spacing and 40% opacity.
- **Data zone.** Columns 10–12.
- **Gates run the full band height**, as 1px silver lines at 28%. The label sits at the top of each gate in a mono caps micro-label (`G9 TABLE SCOPE`), rotated 0°. Labels show their full names on hover or focus, and `G9` alone at rest.

## Rules

1. **The band is never covered.** Panels live on the upper or lower deck. A modal overlay is never used. The only exception is the 390 px console sheet; see `responsive-spec.md`.
2. **One slug in flight at a time.**
   - A new selection cancels the previous slug: it fades over 120 ms and the new one enters.
   - The corpus matrix is the one place where many queries are shown at once. There they become rows of cells, not slugs.
3. **Alignment to gates.** Anything that refers to a gate aligns to its x position:
   - reason strings;
   - corpus matrix columns;
   - the Breach diff hunk.
   The gauntlet is a grid for the whole page.
4. **Left = words, right = rows.** Nothing violates this direction. Even the Builder's chips sit on the upper deck *left*, and its result sits on the *right*.
5. **Negative space is obsidian, never a gradient.** There is no glow, except the 200 ms gate flare and the LIVE dot pulse.

## The Inspector (lower deck), at 1440

There are four columns on a 12-column grid:

1. **Question and assumptions** (3 cols). The Newsreader question, then the assumptions in Instrument Sans, then the cost (RECORDED) and the safety status.
2. **SQL** (4 cols). Two stacked blocks:
   - `as stored · regenerated by the backend` (`wdth` 87, RECORDED). Labelled `proposed by Claude` **only** if the fixture gains a raw `proposed_sql` field.
   - `regenerated from AST · this is what runs` (`wdth` 75, LIVE).
   A diff gutter marks the characters that differ. With today's fixtures, all 10 questions are verified identical. The gutter reads `regenerated again in your browser · identical`, which is the idempotence proof. In the Console, real differences appear, such as `AS` insertion and casing.
3. **Approved views and AST** (3 cols).
   - The 5 views are listed from `schema_catalog.json`. Views referenced by *this* query (from the LIVE `referenced_views`) are lit cobalt, with their columns shown.
   - Below them sits the real AST as a tidy tree. Node count is capped visually at about 40. Deeper trees collapse their subtrees to `+n`.
4. **Result** (2 cols). The table (first 8 rows, scrollable), the row count, and the truncation note.
